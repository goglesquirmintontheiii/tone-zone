Tone Zone **0.2.0**: plugins, Tone Match, a much bigger catalog, and a lighter UI.

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings from 0.1.0 are kept; the new factory presets are added next to them.

### Performance
- **GPU use is way down.** On a 2-core test box, the renderer went from ~40 % to ~11 % on a full board and from ~13 % to ~3 % with one pedal. Every SVG blur/turbulence filter was replaced by pre-rendered shadows, noise patterns and gradient LED glows; the always-on animation loop, CSS transitions on LEDs/meters and per-frame text updates are gone; only values that change are redrawn.
- **Less DSP load:** denormal flushing, faster tanh/sine approximations and cheaper reverb reads (the Brit Crunch preset dropped noticeably).
- Blocks drop onto the board instantly (no more drop-in delay).

### Plugins
- Every "inspired by" brand is now a **built-in plugin** you can switch off in the new **Plugins** tab (61 plugins). Install your own from a .zip or folder (see `docs/plugins.md`).
- Presets that use a block from a disabled plugin keep it as a **gray outline** that passes audio through; turn the plugin back on and the block comes back with its settings.

### Line 6 Helix plugin
- Connect a Helix Floor, LT, Rack, Stomp, Stomp XL or HX Effects over USB: the block shows the matching unit.
- Footswitches, expression pedals, snapshots, tap, tuner and looper over MIDI; recall a Helix preset/snapshot with each Tone Zone preset or follow the Helix.
- Use Tone Zone footswitch/expression blocks as Helix external switches; route the Helix's USB audio in and out of the board.

### Tone Match (new tab)
- Add a song from a file or a **YouTube link** (uses yt-dlp, with a one-click download if it isn't installed).
- Optional **AI track isolation** (Demucs 6-stem: guitar, bass, drums, vocals, piano, other, plus a rhythm/lead guitar estimate). One-click model download; uses your NVIDIA GPU if present, CPU otherwise. A no-AI stereo split is also available.
- Listen to any mix of tracks (mute/solo/volume), pick the part of the song to match.
- **One-click tone replication**: auditions every enabled amp at several gains, measures every cab, predicts all amp × cab pairs and renders the best ones, then dials in the knobs, adds drives, EQ correction and ambience. Or customise it: depth, guitar/bass, character, brands, gear types, and record your own guitar as the test signal. Load the result to the board, A/B it against the song, or save it as a preset.

### Board & UI
- Shift/Ctrl-drag **marquee selection**.
- 3D-looking 1/4" jacks; cables per kind: instrument, TRS, **XLR**, **headphone**, 3.5 mm, control, MIDI, USB. Jacks that don't match won't connect.
- Click **AUDIO IN / OUT** to configure ports (labels, trim, mute, polarity, PipeWire connections). The PipeWire routing panel handles large setups (searchable port picker, no overflow).
- Sidebars are **resizable** and can **pop out** into their own windows and back.
- **Presets tab**: board thumbnails, search, favourites, factory/mine filters, **tags with multi-tag filtering** (match any/all) and a chip tag editor.
- **Browser filters**: type, brand, stereo/mono, effect type, Tone Zone originals, inspired-by; **favourites** with a filter, shown first in each category.
- Speaker cabs take **up to 4 mics**, anywhere, including the left side of a speaker.
- The strobe tuner's display no longer overflows.

### New Tone Zone originals
- **TZ Parametric EQ** (mono + stereo): bigger pedal with an interactive dark screen, 6 bands + HPF/LPF each on a footswitch, **Listen** (solo a band you pick on the screen) and **Spectrum** (pre/post analyser).
- **Stereo pedal** (width, Haas, dual, auto-pan…), **stereo level meter** (vertical bars, level colours, peak hold).
- **Output selectors** 2 / 4 / 6 (stereo in, footswitch-selected stereo outs, one-at-a-time or multi).
- Hard-wired utility boxes: 1→2/4/8 splitters, 2/4/8→1 mergers, stereo↔mono, TRS↔L/R, headphone→L/R, 3.5 mm adapter, polarity inverter.
- DI box, re-amp box, headphone amp, XLR splitter, footswitches and expression pedals with control outputs.
- **87 new originals** across every category (drives, fuzzes, boosts, 7 amps, 7 cabs, preamps, dynamics, EQs, filters, modulation, pitch and wham pedals, wahs, volume pedals, loopers, delays, reverbs, synths, acoustic, multi-FX).

### Engine
- New blocks: routing (split/merge/select/stereo/meter/DI), parametric EQ with listen + spectrum, acoustic, synth, freeze, lo-fi, swell, slicer, external device I/O, diatonic 3-voice harmony.
- Chains can run sub-blocks in parallel, per channel, and with a dry/wet blend; 16 drive voicings.
- Multi-mic cabs, JSON amp/cab voicings for plugins.
