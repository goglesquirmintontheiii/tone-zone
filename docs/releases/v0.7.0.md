Tone Zone **0.7.0**: a real circuit editor, a wiring diagram, scriptable screens and many new parts in the Pedal Factory, plus board fixes.

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.7.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings are kept.

### Circuits
- **Circuit list:** the Circuit tab is now a list of circuits with a picture of each. Search, filter by kind or tag, and see which ones are in the current pedal. Circuits have names, descriptions and tags, and they stay in your library for any pedal.
- **Circuit editor:** New and Edit open a large editor over the canvas.
  - **Building:** drag parts in, then drag from port to port to wire them.
  - **Parts:** about 50 in all: drives, fuzz, filters, EQ, dynamics, modulation, delays, reverbs, pitch, synth, mixers, a feedback loop, plus envelope followers, LFOs, sample and hold, math, a few logic gates and comparators.
  - **Editing:** undo and redo, copy and paste, align, notes and live checks.
  - **Test circuit:** plays the circuit on its own.
- **35 built-in circuits** to start from, including Tube Screamer and Big Muff style circuits, an envelope filter, a ducking delay and a big multi-effect demo.
- **Wiring diagram** under the pedal canvas: drag from a control or jack to a circuit input, or from one circuit to the next. Fold it, resize it, or pop it out into its own window.
- **Several circuits per pedal**, each wired to different controls and jacks, and several controls on one input.
- **One control, many targets:** a knob or switch can drive several things at once, each with its own range.

### Screens you can program
- **Shows:** choose Basic text or Custom for any screen.
- **Custom screens:** run a small, easy Lua-style script. Scripts can draw text, shapes, pixels, meters and icons, make buttons, sliders, lists and text fields, react to taps, read and set knobs, switches and DIP switches, and read signals like the input level.
- **Code editor:** syntax highlighting, error markers, a live preview, examples and an API reference. See [docs/screen-scripts.md](https://github.com/goglesquirmintontheiii/tone-zone/blob/main/docs/screen-scripts.md).
- **Generator:** makes a good-looking screen in one of 8 styles (amp LCD, green phosphor, OLED, colour dashboard, VU meter, tuner, big digits, pixel art) as code you can edit.

### New parts and looks
- **Rotary encoder:** endless, with an optional push switch and an LED ring of 8 to 32 LEDs (arc or dot, one colour or a gradient).
- **New controls:** XY pad, joystick, touch strip, arcade button, step buttons, mini toggle bank, whammy lever, pressure pad, light sensor and needle meter.
- **More variants:** 10 new knob caps, 6 slider looks, 6 rotary switch looks, 10 LED looks and 5 LED bezels.
- **Materials** on foot pedals, the footswitch nut and knob dials.
- **Angled jacks:** jacks follow curves and slanted edges, and cables plug into them properly on the board.
- **Dot grid:** the dots over the enclosure can be turned off per pedal.
- **My pedals:** a library of all your saved pedals to edit, test, duplicate or delete.

### Materials
- **Zebrawood:** redone with long, wavy dark stripes.
- **Transparency:** glass and acrylic have a slider from clear to solid.
- **Colour picker:** hidden when the material doesn't take a free colour.

### Board fixes
- **Zoomed out:** below 50% zoom you can now drag pedals and connect cables.
- **Stuck selection:** a pedal could get stuck selected so nothing else could be clicked. Drags now always end.
- **New controls on the board:** XY pads drag in two directions, encoders turn endlessly, whammy levers and pressure pads spring back, and arcade buttons are on only while held.
- **Rotated pedals:** taps on a rotated pedal's screen land in the right place.

### Engine
- **New `patch` block:** runs any number of circuits wired together as one pedal. Audio can only loop back through a feedback part, which stays bounded.
