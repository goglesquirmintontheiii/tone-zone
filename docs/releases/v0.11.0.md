Tone Zone **0.11.0**: mic blocks with 50 mics, every preset re-miked, themed pedalboards, Helix drag-to-link, hardware I/O in the plugin pop-up, and a feedback tool.

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.11.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings are kept. On a Raspberry Pi, update from the Pi tab.

### Mics
- **Mic blocks:** mics are their own blocks now, in a new Mics section of the library. Drop a mic onto a cab where you want it, then drag it into place on the speakers like before. Drop a mic onto another one to swap the model. Right-click or Delete removes one. A cab starts with a single 57 and takes up to 8 mics.
- **One output per mic:** each mic gets its own jack on the cab, so mics can go to different places (left and right, a different chain). With two or more mics the cab also has a MIX jack first, the blend of all of them, so your existing cables keep working.
- **Cab inspector:** each mic has its model, distance, angle, level and polarity.
- **Core mics (10):** inspired by the SM57, Beta 57A, SM58, MD 421, MD 441, e906, R-121, M 160, U 87 and C414.
- **Studio Mics plugin (40):** 36 more inspired by the studio classics (RE20, SM7B, Beta 52A, D112, Audix i5 and D6, Heil PR 30, M 88, e604, U 47, TLM 103, KM 184, C451, C-800G, ELA M 251, AT4050, NT1, Royer R-10, Coles 4038, RCA 44 and 77-DX, AEA R92 and more) plus four Tone Zone originals: TZ Hornet (heavy rhythm dynamic), TZ Velvet (dark ribbon without the boom), TZ Prism (condenser with the harsh 2 to 3 kHz smoothed) and TZ Room Orb (omni for room sound).
- **Real character:** every mic has its own frequency response, proximity effect (stronger up close, strongest on ribbons), off-axis darkening and level, from the real one. Each one looks like its counterpart too.
- **Every preset re-miked:** all 229 cabs in the factory presets use the new mics, picked by style: a 57 with a 421, a 121 or the TZ Hornet for metal, condensers with ribbons for clean and ambient, ribbons for blues and jazz, a 57 with a 441 or a KM 184 for country and funk, an RE20 or D112 for bass, condensers for acoustic. All presets are level-matched again.
- Old presets and saved blocks load unchanged. If the Studio Mics plugin is off, its mics fall back to the 57.

### Pedalboards
- **Overlapping boards** keep their own pedals: dragging one board over another no longer takes the other board's pedals.
- **Duplicate and copy a board:** Ctrl+D or copy and paste a board brings its pedals, in the same spots, and the cables between them.
- **Themed boards:** the factory presets' boards match their style: wood for acoustic and folk, forged carbon or rusty iron for deathcore, hammertone and gunmetal for metal, pearl and frosted glass for worship, candy acrylic and glow for meme, tweed and tolex for blues and classic rock, and more.
- **Preset images** show the input and output blocks and draw each board in its real materials. They load as you scroll.

### Helix
- **Drag to link:** drag a Helix footswitch onto a pedal's footswitch, or the Helix EXP pedal onto a treadle or knob, to link them. A link works with your real Helix (it uses the Helix's factory messages, and Learn can change them) and when you click the Helix on screen.
- **Link overlays:** linked footswitches and treadles get a faint outline and a tag like "FS1" or "EXP1". Right-click a tag to remove the link; links are also listed in the inspector, and they're saved with the preset and can be undone.

### Plugins page
- **Try a pedal with your guitar:** the pop-up that shows a plugin's pedal now has HARDWARE IN (IN 1, IN 2) and HARDWARE OUT (OUT L, OUT R). Drag cables to the pedal, or click Hook up. While it's hooked up the pop-up gets the audio and your board is paused; close it and the board comes back exactly as it was.

### Presets
- **Lorna Shore Heavy** has a second path: an output selector after the gate picks between the heavy rig and a heavy, dark clean with no distortion at all (a JC-120 and a Twin with lots of headroom, so even the low D1 stays clean).

### Feedback tool
- **Ctrl+Shift+K:** click any element or drag over an area, write a note and press OK. A compact `TZFB1:` string with the context (and a screenshot for an area) is copied, ready to paste into a chat with the developer. It's a temporary development feature.
