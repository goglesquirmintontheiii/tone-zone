Tone Zone **0.8.0**: analog and binary signals in circuits, effects loops in custom pedals, a much richer Pedal Factory, the full Raspberry Pi plan and a preview of the Pi tab, plus new filter pedals.

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.8.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings are kept.

### Circuits and wiring
- **Analog and binary:** "Knob" inputs are now **Analog** inputs (a value from -255 to 255) and "Switch" inputs are now **Binary** inputs (on or off). Circuits can have analog and binary **outputs** too. Old circuits convert automatically.
- **New nodes:** Add, Subtract, Multiply, Divide and Delay (passes on its input after a time you can control).
- **Different wires:** audio is a thick line, analog a thin teal line with diamond ports, binary a dashed amber line with square ports. There's a legend in the editor and the diagram.
- **LEDs:** a circuit's binary output lights an LED, and an analog output sets its brightness, in Test mode and on the board.
- **Stereo jacks:** the PHONES jack shows separate Left and Right ports.
- **Control jacks:** EXP and footswitch jacks work both ways. A circuit can drive another pedal through them, or they can feed a circuit.
- **Circuit to circuit:** any output of one circuit can go into another circuit's input.
- **Screens in the wiring diagram:** each screen can have up to 16 inputs and 16 outputs to and from circuits. Scripts use `input()` and `output()`.
- **Effects loop:** an output jack can be a loop SEND and an input jack a RETURN. On the board you can run other pedals between them, or cable SEND straight to RETURN. It all happens in the same audio cycle, with no added delay.

### Pedal Factory
- **Variant dots:** every control in the parts list has dots for its variants. Click a dot to switch, and every control type has at least 3 variants.
- **LED ring encoder:** it's now an encoder variant, with curved LED segments, and it goes down to 2 LEDs.
- **Folded-in controls:** the toggle bank is now a DIP switch look, and the joystick is an XY pad look.
- **Removed controls:** the light sensor, whammy lever and pressure pad are gone. Designs that used them convert to knobs.
- **Footswitch jack:** plug in an external footswitch.
- **One material panel:** each part has one material panel with a switcher for its pieces (cap, skirt, dial, nut, pedal, grip...). Foot pedals take a material on their body as well as the grip.
- **Colour:** the colour picker now lives only inside the material panel.
- **Drives section removed:** do wiring in the wiring diagram.

### Materials
- **Frosted glass and acrylic** can be tinted any colour.
- **Multicolour** now tints the material instead of replacing it: grain shows through a multicolour stain, and brushing through an anodized fade. It's only offered on finishes that take a colour.

### Raspberry Pi (preview)
- **The plan:** [docs/raspberry-pi.md](https://github.com/goglesquirmintontheiii/tone-zone/blob/main/docs/raspberry-pi.md) lays out the plan for a Pi pedal:
  - Pi 4 and Pi 5, installed with one SSH command;
  - found on the network over Wi-Fi, Ethernet or a direct cable, with no setup knowledge needed;
  - signed updates with rollback, full NAM support and GPIO footswitches;
  - banks of presets switched by footswitches or the screen;
  - USB audio adapters, HATs or the built-in output jack, and headless or with a light screen UI.
- **The Pi tab:** shows the finished UI with demo data. It has devices with an add-a-Pi wizard, presets and banks with a CPU check, a GPIO pin map, display settings with a live preview of the Pi's screen, audio, updates and logs. It doesn't talk to a real Pi yet.

### New pedals
- **Low Pass** and **High Pass:** resonant filter pedals with cutoff shown in Hz.

### Board
- **Pedalboards** can be dragged from anywhere on them at any zoom, including the open gaps on see-through boards.
