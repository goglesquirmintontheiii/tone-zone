Tone Zone **0.4.0**: 669 new blocks (now 1590), complex multi-FX units, 50 new presets based on songs and artists, a NAM capture tab, better cables and plugs, and much smoother big boards.

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.4.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings are kept.

### Removed
- **Tone Match** is gone. It didn't work well enough.
- **The TONE3000 tab** is gone.

### Performance
- **Big boards:** when you zoom out or move around a board with lots of blocks, each block is drawn as a cached image instead of live vector graphics. On a 35 pedal board, panning went from about 15 to about 40 frames per second while using less CPU.
- **Presets tab:** scrolling no longer repaints the whole page every frame. Scroll cost dropped from about 56 ms to 2 ms per frame.
- **Smaller savings:** LEDs, level meters and DSP load badges update less often, and only when something changes.

### Multi-FX
- **43 multi-FX units** in total, with screens, menus and footswitches that work like the real ones. Each has its own screen style and fonts.
- **New inspired-by units:** Line 6 (VX Stomp XL, Rig X Go, and Rig X Express with a working looper), BOSS, Fractal Audio, Neural DSP, HeadRush, Kemper, Zoom, Valeton, Hotone, Mooer, NUX and Tech 21.
- **4 Tone Zone originals:** Atlas, Aurora, PixelQuest and Foundry. Atlas is a large floor unit with pages, sub menus, a row of footswitches and two expression pedals.
- **Realistic I/O:** XLR, headphone, send and return, and expression jacks where the real unit has them.

### Cables and jacks
- **Cable types look different:** XLR cables are thick and matte, headphone cables are thin and coiled, and 1/4" cables are glossy.
- **Better plugs:** longer 1/4" plugs, bigger XLR plugs and slimmer headphone plugs. The metal part sits inside the jack when plugged in. The loose end you drag matches the cable type.
- **Board I/O:** AUDIO IN now has two XLR inputs and a headphone/aux input, and AUDIO OUT has XLR outputs.
- **Couplers:** two tiny new utilities, a double female and a double male 1/4" coupler, with colours you can change. Drag a pedal near the open end of a male coupler and it snaps on, so two pedals can sit right next to each other with no cable.

### Graphics
- **Vacuum tubes:** many tube amps and preamps have small glowing tubes on top that flicker slightly.
- **Toggle switches** are drawn as real levers instead of a circle.
- **DIP switches work** on every pedal that has them, including Cousins.
- **No more overflow:** graphics stay inside each pedal's shape, and detached parts (like on Choir Boy) are joined to the body.
- **Wahs and volume pedals:** all Tone Zone originals now have a bypass footswitch.

### New blocks
- **Inspired-by gear:** 919 pedals, 114 guitar and bass amps, 170 preamps and 73 cabs.
  - Line 6 amps and preamps, many more Fender and TC Electronic pedals, TC Helicon style voice processors, Ampeg pedals, and new gear across most other plugins.
- **70 new Tone Zone originals**, including Perennial, a polyphonic sustain pedal.

### Presets
- **14 song presets:** Sweet Child O' Mine, Smells Like Teen Spirit, Enter Sandman, Master of Puppets, Hysteria, Thunderstruck, Beat It, Layla, Sunshine of Your Love, Nightmare, Holiday, No More Tears, Mr. Brightside and Message in a Bottle.
- **22 artist presets:** Weezer, Metallica, Slipknot, Eric Clapton, The Beatles, Muse, Green Day, Pink Floyd, Avenged Sevenfold, AC/DC, Guns N' Roses, Van Halen, Steve Vai, Polyphia, Linkin Park, Slayer, Slaughter to Prevail, Lorna Shore, Iron Maiden, RHCP, Eagles and Rage Against the Machine.
- **More genres:** grunge, blues, funk, shoegaze, stoner doom, djent, surf and indie, plus four grunge rigs and more synth and meme presets.
- **Delete built-in presets** from the Presets tab (hover a card, or use Select to delete many). Restore built-in brings them back. Built-in presets you haven't changed now update with the app.
- **Fixed:** loading a preset from another tab no longer leaves the view far away from the blocks.

### Plugins tab
- **Redesigned:** each plugin has a card with a picture of its blocks, a short description and block counts.
- **Details:** click a card to see every block it adds. Click a block to open it on its own and try the controls without changing your board.
- **Remove built-in plugins** too, with a Restore button.

### Capture
- **Make NAM models of your presets.** The Capture tab plays a test signal through the current preset and trains a Neural Amp Modeler model from it. Training needs the NAM trainer installed; Settings can set it up.

### Raspberry Pi
- **New tab** for flashing presets onto a Raspberry Pi. It's a placeholder for now.

### Other
- **Simpler wording** across the app, and no more long dashes.
- **Engine:** extra outputs (XLR, headphones) get the main signal.
