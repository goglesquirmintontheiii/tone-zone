Tone Zone **0.9.0**: the first working Raspberry Pi version, showcase templates with coded screens, real circuit boards in clear pedals, direct wiring and fan-out, endless encoders and a screen events system.

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.9.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings are kept.

### Raspberry Pi (first version)
**Not yet tested on a real Pi.** It was tested here with an emulated ARM engine and simulated Pi hardware. Please report what you find.

- **Install:** on a Pi 3B/3B+, 4 or 5 running 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS (Bookworm), install with one command. The `pi-bundle` and `install.sh` are attached to this release; exact steps are in [docs/raspberry-pi.md](https://github.com/goglesquirmintontheiii/tone-zone/blob/main/docs/raspberry-pi.md).
- **Engine:** the same engine as the desktop app, built for ARM, with full NAM support. Under emulation it renders the same as the desktop build.
- **Pairing:** the Pi tab finds Pis on the network or over a direct cable. Pair with a 6-digit code.
- **Presets:** push presets in banks; NAM models and IRs go along with them.
- **Switching:** change presets instantly from GPIO footswitches (press, hold for bank, debounced), from the Pi's screen, or from the desktop.
- **LEDs:** GPIO LEDs, on/off or dimmable (hardware or software PWM).
- **Status:** DSP load, CPU load and temperature are shown live.
- **Audio devices:** pick input and output devices separately, for example a USB adapter in and the 3.5 mm jack out.
- **Updates:** signed update bundles over the network or a cable, with automatic rollback if an update fails.
- **Screen UI:** an optional, very light screen UI: presets, blocks with big sliders, tuner and meters.

### Pi tab
- **Load bars:** DSP load with a CPU load bar under it, both turning green, yellow, then red. Each Pi has an adjustable DSP budget.
- **Temperature:** the reading turns red around 77 °C and flashes above 80 °C.
- **Boards:** Pi 3B/3B+ supported, including its built-in 3.5 mm output.
- **PWM LEDs:** LED pins can be set to PWM brightness.
- **Devices:** click a Pi's card to select it, and drag cards to reorder them.

### Pedal Factory
- **5 new templates** with coded screens: VU Drive, Wave Trem, Squeeze, and two advanced ones.
  - **Command Center:** a five-effect multi with tabbed pages, dials, menus and animations.
  - **Pattern Lab:** an 8-step filter sequencer with a drag-to-edit grid.
- **Synth Box** now draws its real waveform from the knobs, switches and DIPs. **Twin Echo** has an animated screen.
- **Circuit boards** in clear pedals are built from the pedal's actual circuits: chips, parts and traces. Screens get a ribbon cable of 12 to 24 wires, and step buttons get a wire each.

### Wiring diagram
- **Direct links:** wire controls and jacks straight to LEDs, out jacks, screens and other controls, with no circuit needed.
- **Fan-out:** one source can feed many places. Drag again from the same port.
- **Screens** can be moved around.
- **RGB and bicolour LEDs** have one input per colour.
- **LED rings** on encoders can be driven from the diagram. When nothing is wired, they show the encoder's position.
- **Endless encoders:** they spin endlessly and pulse CW or CCW outputs. Choose round and round, stop at the ends, or step counting.

### Screen code
- **Events:** `on(event, name, fn)` runs a function when things happen: footswitches pressed or released, encoders turned (with direction), controls changed (old and new value), screen inputs changed, DIPs, buttons, touches, bypass, tap tempo and preset loads.
- **Hints while you type:** autocomplete, signature help, hover help and gentle warnings.
- **Docs** for reading screen inputs and writing screen outputs from the wiring diagram.

### Engine
- **Preset preloading:** presets can be preloaded for instant switching (used by the Pi).
