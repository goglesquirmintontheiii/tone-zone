Tone Zone **0.3.0**: a much smoother UI, fixed audio I/O editing, Helix foot control of other pedals, and 106 new blocks (synths, memes, Chase Bliss, Big Ear and boutique gear).

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.3.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings are kept.

### Performance
- **Block list sidebar:** it's now virtualized and shows cached pedal images instead of live vector drawings, so scrolling through all 921 blocks stays smooth.
- **Presets tab:** board thumbnails are pre-rendered images. With the tab open and nothing happening, renderer CPU went from about 62 % to about 8 % on the 2-core test box.
- **Pan and zoom:** moving the board is a cheap GPU transform, and the full redraw happens a few times a second at most. The background grid is its own layer.
- **Meters:** the level meters draw on a canvas, so they no longer repaint the page every frame.
- **Smaller savings:** LED glows and meter segments are cheaper to change, and off-screen blocks are skipped. The For The Love Of God board's renderer use went from about 18 % to about 11 %.
- **Thumbnail cache:** pedal images are cached on disk. They appear instantly after the first launch and are rebuilt automatically when the app updates.

### Fixes
- **Adding or removing inputs and outputs** in Settings or the AUDIO IN / OUT panel works again. The lockup when you opened them is gone. It was an endless refresh loop, and the engine now reports PipeWire port changes on its own.
- **You can type in the YouTube link box** in Tone Match again (Enter starts the download).
- **Overlapping text fixed** on about 30 pedals (EHX, TC Electronic, MXR, NUX, Strymon, ProCo, Grace Design, LR Baggs, EarthQuaker, Big Ear, JHS). On a few of these the jacks moved, so existing cables plug in at the new spot.

### Line 6 Helix: control other pedals
- **Bind controls to other pedals:** a new **Control other pedals** section in the Helix block's inspector binds any Helix footswitch or expression pedal to a footswitch or treadle of any other pedal on the board.
- **Learn:** press Learn, then move the Helix control you want to use.
- **Footswitch modes:** footswitches can toggle (press) or act momentarily (hold).
- **Saved with presets:** bindings are stored in the preset.

### Engine
- **New algorithms:**
  - `vocal`: a formant voice with talk, sing, meow, fart, robot and choir modes plus lyric syllables.
  - `lossy`: spectral codec damage.
  - `microloop`: a short-term memory looper.
- **Chains:** sub-blocks that start switched off are silent.
- **Ports:** port lists update from PipeWire callbacks (debounced).
