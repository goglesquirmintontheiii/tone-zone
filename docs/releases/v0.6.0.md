Tone Zone **0.6.0**: the Pedal Factory now builds working pedals, and pedalboards and materials look much more real.

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.6.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings are kept.

### Pedal Factory: build real pedals
- **Save as block:** a finished design becomes a real block. It shows up under the new **Custom** filter in the block list and works on the board with all its controls and jacks.
- **Circuit tab:** 24 one-click starter circuits (boost, overdrive, fuzz, compressor, chorus, phaser, flanger, tremolo, vibe, delay, tape echo, reverb, shimmer, octave, wah, envelope filter, shoegaze stack, ambient, parallel blend, synth, lo-fi and more). Each one sets up the sound and the matching controls.
  - **Advanced:** reorder stages, run stages in parallel with a blend, pick the clipping or compressor type, set fixed values, and add a wet/dry mix.
  - **Signal flow view:** click a stage to turn it off.
- **Wiring:** every control can drive any stage value (with range, taper and invert), a stage on/off, the mix, tap tempo or bypass. LEDs can follow the pedal, a control, or the delay tempo.
- **Test mode:** play your guitar through the design and turn the knobs before saving. In and out meters show the level.
- **DIP switches:** up to 12 switches, each controlling something different.
- **New controls:** trim pot, slider, rotary switch and a foot pedal (treadle).
- **New jacks:** headphones and XLR out.
- **8 new shapes:** hexagon, octagon, oval, flare, ticket, tower, waist and drop (16 in total).
- **Multi-select:** Shift or Ctrl click, or drag a box, to edit several parts at once. Align and space-out buttons too.
- **Mirror mode** (M): parts get a mirrored twin that follows every move and resize.
- **Circuit board:** clear glass and acrylic enclosures can show a circuit board with wires to every control, in the factory and on the board.
- **7 new templates**, all wired and ready to play.
- **Fixed:** LEDs and screens can be selected after you drop them.
- **Panning:** dragging empty space now draws a selection box. Pan with Space-drag, the middle button or right-drag.

### Pedalboards
- **XL Max:** 6 rails (three rows of 10, 9 and 9 spots).
- **Frame:** one solid piece now, with no overlapping corners.
- **Glass and acrylic:** see-through boards leave the space between the rails empty.
- **Glass:** glass frames have no screws. The screw mountings are switched off for them.
- **Taller pedals:** pedals a little taller than a row, like the level meter, stand on that row's two rails.

### Materials
- **Woods:** much more realistic, with each species having its own grain, pores, figure and colour (new: quilted maple). Nitro, poly and matte finishes look different.
- **Metals:** hammered, patina and rust textures are reworked and more random.
- **Wear:** Scratched and Chipped now go from barely there to heavy. Lightly scratched and Lightly chipped are gone, and saved designs that used them still load.
- **New finish:** Flake on carbon fibre, which looks like forged carbon.
- **Mother of pearl** and abalone look better.
- **First draw:** a new material takes up to about half a second to draw the first time it's used in a session.

### Other
- **Block list:** the Source filter has a Custom option.
- **Engine:** chain blocks can route each output to left, right or a mono mix, so custom pedals with headphone and XLR jacks get the right channels.
