Tone Zone **0.5.0**: pedalboards with real materials, block rotation, a Pedal Factory, app themes, level-matched presets and a round of board fixes.

### Install
Download `Tone-Zone-0.5.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and run. Needs PipeWire's JACK library (default on Nobara; otherwise `sudo dnf install pipewire-jack-audio-connection-kit-libs`). Your presets, library and settings are kept.

### Pedalboards
- **Six sizes:** Tiny (4 pedals), Small (8), Medium (12), Large (18), XL (24) and XL Max (28), in the new Pedalboards section of the block list.
- **Snapping:** drop a pedal on a board and it snaps into the nearest free spot. Drag the frame and every pedal on it comes along. Floor units, amps and cabs stay off.
- **Names:** right-click a board and pick Rename. The name shows above it.
- **Mounting:** full velcro, velcro strips (one per pedal spot), screws, messy (crooked and partly stripped screws) or blank. Velcro comes in black, white or semi-clear.
- **Materials:** the frame, rails and screws each get their own material: metal, wood, plastic, glass, acrylic, carbon fibre, tolex / leather, stone, rubber, glow in the dark, mother of pearl and concrete. Each material has its own types (14 woods, 10 metals and more), finishes (brushed, patina, nitro, poly and so on) and colours or stains.
- **Multicolour:** fades, bursts (sunburst, cherry, tobacco, blue), gradients, two-tone, colour shift and rainbow. Wood grain and brushing show through.
- **Wear:** stack scratches, rust, chips, fading, relic wear, stains and dents, each with its own amount.

### Board
- **Rotate blocks** in 90 degree steps: press R (Shift+R the other way) or right-click. Cables follow the jacks.
- **Couplers:** a male coupler turns by itself to fit a jack on the top or bottom of a pedal. Moving a group with a coupler in it no longer knocks it out of line, and the coupler now sits inside the pedal's jack instead of under it.
- **Plugs:** 1/4" and headphone plugs sit right up against the jack nut, with no gap.
- **Fixed:** after loading a preset, blocks were invisible until you zoomed in past 50% or changed something.
- **Zoom:** the board stays sharp vector graphics at 50% and above. Below 50% blocks are drawn as cached images to keep big boards smooth.
- **Tubes** on tube amps and preamps glow much brighter.
- **Fixed:** knob value tips could stay on screen after moving the board or switching tabs.

### Presets
- **Same volume:** every factory preset is matched to the same loudness (-17 LUFS), so switching presets no longer jumps in volume. Nothing clips any more.
- **Overall level:** presets are about 7 dB quieter on average than before, which leaves room for clean, peaky rigs. Turn up your interface or monitors a little if needed.
- Built-in presets you haven't changed update automatically.

### Pedal Factory (new tab, design only)
- **Your own pedal design:** pick the enclosure size and shape, then add knobs, switches, buttons, footswitches, LEDs, screens, DIP switches, jacks, text and artwork.
- **Materials:** every part can have its own material, finish and wear, like a wood knob next to a glow-in-the-dark one.
- **Patterns:** stack enclosure patterns: stripes, checkers, swirl, fisheye, polka dots, chevron, gradient, camo, marble, galaxy, tie-dye, plaid or your own image.
- **Editing:** undo and redo, layers, templates and grid snapping.
- **Coming later:** sound, and saving your design as a block.

### App
- **Themes:** Settings has Dark, White and Monochrome. Only the app's colours change, not the pedals.
- **Plugins tab:** choose Large, Medium, Tile or List view. Screens, knobs and switches all work when you open a block from a plugin.
- **New block:** Easel Tuner (inspired by the Walrus Audio Canvas Tuner), with needle and strobe views and eight colour themes, plus the Easel Mono DI.
